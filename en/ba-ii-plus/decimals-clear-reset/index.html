BA II Plus basics: decimal places, clearing memory, reset, and AOS vs Chain

To show 4 decimals press 2NDFORMAT. (FORMAT), type 4, press ENTER, then 2NDQUITCPT (QUIT). The calculator keeps all digits; only the display changes.

AOS vs Chain: why 3 + 4 × 5 gives 35

After a reset the BA II Plus works in Chn (chain) mode: each operation is done as soon as you press the next one, left to right. In AOS it follows the usual order (multiplication before addition).

Storing a number

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
11.0525STO11.05Store a number in memory 1 (M0 to M9).
2CE|CCE|C0.00Clear the display.
3RCL11.05Recall it (all digits are kept).

STO and a digit store the display in one of ten memories; RCL and the digit bring it back with every digit, which is better than retyping a rounded number.

Clearing: which key clears what

KeysWhat it clears
CE|CThe number being typed or an error message; a second press clears the calculation in progress. Not the TVM values, not the worksheets.
2NDCLR TVMFVCLR TVM: N, I/Y, PV, PMT and FV (P/Y, C/Y and BGN are kept).
2NDCLR WORKCE|CCLR WORK: the worksheet you are in (cash flows, bond, amortization...).
2NDMEM0 then 2NDCLR WORKCE|CThe ten memories M0 to M9.
2NDRESET+|−ENTERRESET: everything, including P/Y, BGN, decimals and AOS.

Reset

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
12NDRESET+|−RST ? 0.00RESET asks for confirmation.
2ENTERRST 0.00Everything back to factory settings: memories, worksheets, P/Y = 1, END, 2 decimals, Chn.

A reset is the quickest way to a known state before an exam or when nothing adds up. Afterwards set what you need again (decimals, AOS, P/Y). See BA II Plus for the CFA exam.
