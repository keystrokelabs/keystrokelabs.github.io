Check these before any problem

  • P/Y (2NDP/YI/Y shows it, 2NDQUITCPT quits): 12 for monthly problems with a yearly rate, or 1 if you enter everything per period.
  • END or BGN: BGN on the display means payments at the start of each period; 2NDBGNPMT2NDSETENTER2NDQUITCPT switches.
  • Cleared: 2NDCLR TVMFV (CLR TVM) before TVM, CF 2NDCLR WORKCE|C before cash flows.
  • Signs: money received positive, money paid negative (+|− is the +|− key).
