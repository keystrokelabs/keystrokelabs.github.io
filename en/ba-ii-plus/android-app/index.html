A BA II Plus-style financial calculator for Android: ba ii Pro

Works on: BA II Plus and BA II Plus Professional

ba ii Pro is our Android app, built on the same engine that checks every BA II Plus page on this site. It follows the BA II Plus Professional: the same keys, the 2nd functions and the worksheets.

What it does

  • TVM with P/Y, C/Y and BGN; amortization; cash flows with NPV, IRR, NFV, payback, discounted payback and modified IRR; bonds with accrued interest and modified duration; depreciation (SL, SYD, DB, DBX); statistics and regressions; interest conversion; dates; profit margin; breakeven; percent change; ten memories; AOS and Chain.
  • A history of every calculation, and built-in help.
  • Practice mode: nine short money stories you key in yourself, with hints and a checked answer (sign included).
  • Tools: a TVM summary, an amortization schedule and a cash-flow diagram.
  • Checked against 42 tables of worked examples from the guidebook and every error condition (how we check).

What is free

The calculator, every key and worksheet, the history and help are free, with ads. A one-time upgrade (not a subscription) removes the ads and unlocks all drills (the first is free), the complete amortization schedule and cash-flow diagram, and quick-switch settings (every setting can also be changed with the keys for free). The full comparison is on the apps page.

What it is not

  • Not made by or affiliated with Texas Instruments: an independent app inspired by the calculator.
  • No French depreciation methods (SLF, DBF): there are no published examples to check them against.
  • Not allowed in exams: no phone is. Take the real calculator.
  • Available on Android, in English.
