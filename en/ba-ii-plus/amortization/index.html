BA II Plus amortization: interest, principal and balance between two payments (P1, P2)

Short answer

Compute the payment first, then press 2NDAMORTPV (AMORT), enter the first and last payment of the range as P1 and P2, and press ↓ to read BAL, PRN and INT. Year 1 here: interest INT= -14,916.50, principal PRN= -3,070.06.

Numbers checked on 2026-11-23Works on: BA II Plus and BA II Plus Professional

📱 Try these keys on your phone: ba ii Pro, free on Android →

The AMORT worksheet (2NDAMORTPV) splits a range of payments into principal and interest and gives the balance after the range. It reads the loan from the TVM keys, so compute (or enter) the payment first, with P/Y set as for the loan.

Example: years 1 and 2 of a mortgage

A 250,000 mortgage at 6% a year, 30 years, monthly payments. How much of the first year's payments is interest, and what is left to pay?

Rounding

For AMORT, the BA II Plus uses the payment rounded to the number of decimals on the display (a lender pays in cents), so set 2 decimals for money. With more decimals the figures change by a few cents. FV computed in TVM uses the unrounded payment.

One payment at a time

Set P1 and P2 to the same number, for example 1 and 1, to see a single payment; CPT at P1 then moves to payment 2, 3 and so on.
