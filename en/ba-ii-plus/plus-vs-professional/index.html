BA II Plus vs BA II Plus Professional: the real differences, and which one you need

Short answer

Same keys and same answers for TVM, amortization, NPV, IRR, bonds and statistics. The Professional adds NFV, payback, discounted payback and modified IRR in the cash-flow worksheet, modified duration in the bond worksheet, and room for 32 cash flows instead of 24. If your course uses payback or MIRR, it saves time; otherwise the Plus does the same job.

What the extra keys do: one project, four answers

A project costs 50,000 today and returns 15,000 in year 1, 18,000 in each of years 2 to 4 and 20,000 in year 5. At 10%, the NPV is NPV= 16,748.73 on both models. The Professional then shows the NFV (NFV= 26,974.00), the payback (PB= 2.94 years) and the discounted payback (DPB= 3.65 years) by scrolling down the same worksheet:

On the plain BA II Plus, steps 11-13 do not exist. You would add the flows by hand until they cover the cost: 15,000 + 18,000 = 33,000 after year 2, so 17,000 is left out of year 3's 18,000, or about 2.94 years. The same idea with discounted flows gives the discounted payback.

Which one should you get?

  • BA II Plus: enough for TVM, loans, NPV, IRR and bonds, which is what most courses and exam questions use.
  • BA II Plus Professional: worth it if payback, discounted payback, MIRR or duration come up often (corporate finance, CFA, FRM), or if you have more than 24 cash flows.
  • Everything on this site works the same on both.
