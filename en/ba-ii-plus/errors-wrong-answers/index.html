BA II Plus giving wrong answers? Error 1 to 8 and the usual causes

Press CE|C to clear an error. Most "wrong" answers come from four things: values left from an earlier problem (use 2NDCLR TVMFV), P/Y not what you think (2NDP/YI/Y shows it), BGN left on, and signs (money paid must be negative).

Works on: BA II Plus and BA II Plus Professional · Numbers checked on 2026-11-23

When the answer is wrong but there is no error

Error messages

Clear any error with CE|C. The values you entered are kept.

MessageMeaningCommon causes
Error 1OverflowA result beyond ±9.9999999999999E99; division by zero (also inside a calculation); 1/x of 0; statistics with all X or Y values the same.
Error 2Invalid argumentx! of a non-integer or above 69; LN of 0 or less; square root of a negative; AMORT with P2 smaller than P1; depreciation with salvage above cost.
Error 3Too many pending operationsMore than 15 levels of parentheses or 8 pending operations.
Error 4Out of rangeP/Y or C/Y of 0 or less; P1/P2 outside 1-9,999; a cash-flow frequency outside 0.5-9,999 (the guide's range); DEC outside 0-9; a date outside 1980-2079.
Error 5No solution existsCPT I/Y with PV, PMT and FV all the same sign; IRR with no sign change in the cash flows.
Error 6Invalid dateA date that does not exist or is in the wrong format (MM.DDYY); a bond redemption date on or before settlement.
Error 7Iteration limit exceededI/Y, IRR or YLD too complex to solve (often cash flows that change sign several times). Check the inputs; for IRR, look at NPV at a few rates instead.
Error 8CanceledA key was pressed while I/Y, IRR, YLD, BAL or a depreciation value was being computed.
