NPV and IRR on the BA II Plus with uneven cash flows (CF, frequencies)

Short answer

Press CF then 2NDCLR WORKCE|C (CLR WORK), enter CF0 (negative for an investment), each later flow as C01, C02... with its frequency F01, F02..., then NPV, the rate as I, ↓ CPT: NPV= 16,748.73. IRR CPT: IRR= 21.84%.

Work it out with your own numbers ↓

Works on: BA II Plus and BA II Plus Professional

📱 Try these keys on your phone: ba ii Pro, free on Android →

The BA II Plus keeps cash flows in the CF worksheet: CF0 at time 0, then up to 24 more flows on the BA II Plus (C01 to C24; 32 on the Professional), each with a frequency (F01, F02...) for flows that repeat in a row. NPV and IRR read that list.

Example: NPV and IRR of a project

A project costs 50,000 today and returns 15,000 in year 1, 18,000 in each of years 2 to 4, and 20,000 in year 5. What are its NPV at 10% and its IRR?

Things that catch people

  • CE|C does not clear cash flows. Only CLR WORK inside the CF worksheet does. Old flows beyond the ones you entered stay in the list and change NPV and IRR.
  • Frequency, not year. F02 = 3 means "C02 three times in a row", not "in year 3". A year with no cash flow is a flow of 0, entered like any other.
  • Error 5 from IRR means the flows never change sign; Error 7 means the search gave up (flows that change sign several times). See BA II Plus errors.
  • On the BA II Plus Professional, the NPV worksheet also gives NFV, payback (PB), discounted payback (DPB) and modified IRR (MOD); the keys above are the same on both models.
