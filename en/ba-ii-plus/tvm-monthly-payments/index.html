BA II Plus TVM: PV, FV, PMT and monthly payments without the wrong answer (P/Y, C/Y, BGN)

Short answer

Clear TVM, set P/Y to 12 for monthly payments, enter N (months), I/Y (the yearly rate) and PV, then CPT PMT: PMT= -497.39.

2NDCLR TVMFV·2NDP/YI/Y·12ENTER·2NDQUITCPT·60N·7.2I/Y·25000PV·CPTPMT

Work it out with your own numbers ↓

Works on: BA II Plus and BA II Plus Professional

📱 Try these keys on your phone: ba ii Pro, free on Android →

The BA II Plus solves loans and savings in its TVM keys: N, I/Y, PV, PMT, FV. Enter four, then press CPT and the fifth. Three settings decide whether the answer is right: P/Y (payments per year), C/Y (compounding periods per year) and END/BGN. Guides disagree about what P/Y "starts at"; the calculator answers for itself: 2NDP/YI/Y shows it.

Example: monthly payment on a loan

You borrow 25,000 at 7.2% a year, compounded monthly, for 5 years, with a payment at the end of each month.

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
12NDCLR TVMFV0.00CLR TVM: N, I/Y, PV, PMT and FV back to 0.
22NDP/YI/YP/Y= 1.00P/Y: the display shows the current payments per year. Check it.
312ENTERP/Y= 12.00Set 12 payments per year (C/Y follows: 12).
42NDQUITCPT0.00QUIT: back to the calculator.
560NN= 60.005 years × 12 = 60 payments.
67.2I/YI/Y= 7.20The yearly rate in percent. With P/Y = 12 the calculator divides by 12 itself.
725000PVPV= 25,000.00Loan received, positive.
8CPTPMTPMT= -497.39The monthly payment (negative: you pay it).

The payment is PMT= -497.39, negative because it is money you pay. Step 2 is the one that matters: if the display shows P/Y= 1.00, the rest of the keys give a very different number.

Future value of monthly deposits

You deposit 200 at the end of each month for 10 years at 5% a year.

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
12NDCLR TVMFV0.00CLR TVM: N, I/Y, PV, PMT and FV back to 0.
22NDP/YI/YP/Y= 1.00P/Y: the display shows the current payments per year. Check it.
312ENTERP/Y= 12.00Set 12 payments per year (C/Y follows: 12).
42NDQUITCPT0.00QUIT: back to the calculator.
5120NN= 120.0010 years of monthly deposits.
65I/YI/Y= 5.005% a year.
7200+|−PMTPMT= -200.00Each deposit, negative.
8CPTFVFV= 31,056.46Balance after the last deposit.

You end up with FV= 31,056.46. If the deposits are made at the start of each month (BGN):

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
12NDCLR TVMFV0.00CLR TVM: N, I/Y, PV, PMT and FV back to 0.
22NDP/YI/YP/Y= 1.00P/Y: the display shows the current payments per year. Check it.
312ENTERP/Y= 12.00Set 12 payments per year (C/Y follows: 12).

62NDSETENTERBGNSET switches to BGN (BGN appears on the display).

95I/YI/Y= 5.005% a year.

BGN gives FV= 31,185.86. BGN stays on until you switch it back, even after the calculator is turned off, and CLR TVM does not touch it; the last step puts it back to END.

Monthly payments with semiannual compounding (C/Y)

Canadian fixed-rate mortgages compound twice a year but are paid monthly. Set P/Y to 12 and then C/Y to 2 (entering P/Y always sets C/Y to the same value, so C/Y comes second). A 400,000 mortgage at 5% over 25 years:

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
12NDCLR TVMFV0.00CLR TVM: N, I/Y, PV, PMT and FV back to 0.
22NDP/YI/YP/Y= 1.00P/Y.
312ENTERP/Y= 12.0012 payments a year.
4↓2ENTERC/Y= 2.00C/Y: interest compounded twice a year.
52NDQUITCPT0.00QUIT.
6300NN= 300.0025 years × 12.
75I/YI/Y= 5.005% a year, compounded semiannually.
8400000PVPV= 400,000.00The mortgage.
9CPTPMTPMT= -2,326.42Monthly payment.

The payment is PMT= -2,326.42. Leaving C/Y at 12 would give PMT= -2,338.36.

P/Y = 1: the exam-style setting

Many textbooks and exam prep courses keep P/Y and C/Y at 1 and enter everything per period: N in months and I/Y as the monthly rate (0.6I/Y for 7.2% a year). 2NDP/YI/Y1ENTER2NDQUITCPT60N0.6I/Y25000PVCPTPMT gives PMT= -497.39, the same payment, with no hidden setting. Either way works; mixing them is what gives wrong answers.
