BA II Plus for the CFA exam: a settings check, and Plus vs Professional

Reset (2NDRESET+|−ENTER), set 4 decimals and AOS in FORMAT, then look at P/Y (2NDP/YI/Y) and END/BGN (2NDBGNPMT) so you know your starting point. Both the BA II Plus and the Professional are on CFA Institute's calculator list; check the current policy before your exam.

Works on: BA II Plus and BA II Plus Professional · Numbers checked on 2026-11-23

Why each step: the reset removes anything left from practice (cash flows, BGN, a P/Y of 12). Four decimals avoid rounding a rate too early. AOS makes 3+4×5= give 23 as written; in the default Chain mode it gives 35. P/Y is the setting behind most wrong TVM answers: whichever value you use, use it on purpose (see TVM and P/Y).

During the exam

  • Start every TVM problem with 2NDCLR TVMFV (CLR TVM) and every cash-flow problem with CF 2NDCLR WORKCE|C (CLR WORK).
  • Glance at the display for BGN before pressing CPT.
  • Semiannual bonds: N and I/Y per half-year, and double the computed yield (bond price and YTM).
  • Store intermediate results with STO instead of retyping rounded numbers.

Everything on this site works the same on both. The Professional saves time on payback and MIRR questions; the Plus is enough for the rest.

Is it allowed?

CFA Institute publishes which calculators may be used; at the time of writing its policy names the BA II Plus (including the Professional) and the HP 12C (including the Platinum and some special editions). The policy can change: read the CFA exam calculator policy for your exam date. No phone or app is allowed in the exam.
