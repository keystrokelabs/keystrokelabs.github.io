NPV and IRR on the HP 12C with uneven cash flows (CFo, CFj, Nj)

Clear everything with f CLEAR REG, enter the initial flow with g CFo (negative for an investment), each later flow with g CFj (g Nj repeats one), the rate in i, then f NPV (16,748.73) and f IRR (21.84%).

fCLEAR REGCLx·50000CHSgPVCF₀·15000gPMTCFj·18000gPMTCFj·3gFVNj·20000gPMTCFj·10i·fNPVPV·fIRRFV

Works on: HP 12C, 12C Gold and 12C Platinum in RPN mode · Numbers checked on 2026-11-23

The HP 12C keeps cash flows in its storage registers: gPVCF₀ (CFo) stores the flow at time 0, each gPMTCFj (CFj) stores the next one, and gFVNj (Nj) says how many times in a row the last one repeats. fNPVPV then gives the NPV at the rate in i, and fIRRFV the IRR.

Example: NPV and IRR of a project

A project costs 50,000 today and returns 15,000 in year 1, 18,000 in each of years 2 to 4, and 20,000 in year 5. Is it worth it at a 10% cost of capital, and what is its IRR?

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
1fCLEAR REGCLx0.00CLEAR REG: erase old cash flows and registers.
250000CHSgPVCF₀-50,000.00CF0: the investment today, negative.
315000gPMTCFj15,000.00CF1: year 1.
418000gPMTCFj18,000.00CF2: year 2.
53gFVNj3.00Nj: the 18,000 happens 3 times in a row (years 2, 3 and 4).
620000gPMTCFj20,000.00The flow of year 5.
710i10.00Discount rate: 10% per period.

The NPV at 10% is 16,748.73: positive, so the project earns more than 10%. The IRR is 21.84% a year. Both are "per period": with monthly flows, i and the IRR are monthly rates.

Checking what you entered

The HP 12C reviews flows from the last one back: RCLgFVNj shows the Nj of flow n, and RCLgPMTCFj shows flow n and lowers n by one. Because n goes down, store the number of flows in n again when you finish.

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
1RCLn3.00How many flows (groups) follow CF0: 3 (15,000; 18,000 × 3; 20,000).
2RCLgFVNj1.00Nj of the last flow.
3RCLgPMTCFj20,000.00The last flow; n drops to 2.
4RCLgFVNj3.00Nj of flow 2.
5RCLgPMTCFj18,000.00Flow 2; n drops to 1.
63n3.00Put the count back before computing NPV or IRR.

To correct a flow, store the new amount in its register: 16000STO2 replaces flow 2. If an Nj or the order is wrong, the simplest fix is fCLEAR REGCLx and entering the flows again.

Limits and messages

  • Up to 20 flows after CF0 (Nj groups count as one), each repeated up to 99 times. More than that gives Error 6.
  • Error 7 from IRR: the flows do not change sign (every flow positive, or every one negative).
  • Error 3 from IRR: the calculator could not find an answer, which can happen when the flows change sign more than once. Key a guess for the rate and press RCLgR/SPSE to search from it.
