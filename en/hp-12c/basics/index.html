HP 12C basics: ENTER (RPN), clearing, decimal places and percent

Key the first number, press ENTER, key the second number, then the operation. (3 + 4) × 5 is 35.00, with no = key and no parentheses.

3ENTER·4+·5×

Works on: HP 12C, 12C Gold and 12C Platinum in RPN mode · Numbers checked on 2026-11-23

The HP 12C has no = key. It uses RPN (Reverse Polish Notation): you give it the numbers first and the operation last. Once that clicks, long calculations take fewer keys than on an ordinary calculator, and you never need parentheses.

Two numbers and an operation: ENTER

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
13ENTER3.00Key 3 and press ENTER to separate it from the next number.
24+7.003 + 4: the operation comes after both numbers.
35×35.00The 7 is still there: key 5 and press ×. No ENTER needed after a result.

ENTER separates two numbers you key one after the other. After an operation the result is already waiting, so the next number goes straight in: (3 + 4) × 5 needs one ENTER. To make a number negative, key it and press CHS: 250CHS shows -250.00.

Percent: %, Δ% and %T

% takes a percentage of the number you keyed before it, and leaves that number in place, so + or − right after adds or removes it.

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
1200ENTER200.00The base amount.
215%30.0015% of 200. The 200 stays in the calculator.
3+230.00200 + 15%: the price with the increase.

200 plus 15% is 230.00. For a 10% discount, key 200ENTER10%−: 180.00.

The increase is 30.00%. %T gives the share of a total:

120 out of 480 is 25.00% of the total.

Decimal places

f followed by a digit sets how many decimals the display shows. The calculator always keeps the full number (about 10 digits); only the display is rounded.

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
12ENTER3÷0.672 ÷ 3 with the usual 2 decimals.
2f40.6667f 4: show 4 decimals.
3f20.67f 2: back to 2. The number inside never changed.

Use 2 decimals for money and 4 for rates. The one exception is AMORT, which uses the rounded figures shown on the display (see loan payment and amortization).

Clearing

KeysWhat it clears
CLxThe number on the display only (to correct a typo).
fCLEAR FINx≷yCLEAR FIN: the financial registers n, i, PV, PMT and FV. Use it before every loan or savings problem.
fCLEAR REGCLxCLEAR REG: every number (stack, storage registers, financial registers, cash flows). Use it before entering cash flows for NPV and IRR.
fCLEAR ΣSSTCLEAR Σ: the statistics registers and the stack.
fCLEAR PRGMR↓CLEAR PRGM: in program mode, the program memory.

None of these turns BEGIN off or changes the number of decimals. Look at the display: if BEGIN is showing and your payments are at the end of each period, press g8END.

Point or comma as the decimal mark

To switch between 1,234.56 and 1.234,56: turn the calculator off, hold down the • key, press ON, and release •. Do it again to switch back. Whichever style you choose, the decimal point is always keyed with the • key.

Starting over completely

To reset everything (all registers, programs and settings): turn the calculator off, hold down −, press ON, release −. The display shows Pr Error; press any key. This is the same "Continuous Memory" reset that happens when the batteries run out.
