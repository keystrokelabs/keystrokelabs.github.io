HP 12C error codes: what Error 0 to 9 and Pr Error mean, and the fix

Press any key to clear the message (the numbers stay). The most common one, Error 5, means the financial problem has no answer, almost always because PV, PMT and FV all have the same sign: make the money you pay negative with CHS.

Works on: HP 12C, 12C Gold and 12C Platinum in RPN mode · Numbers checked on 2026-11-23

The HP 12C shows a short message instead of a number when it cannot do what you asked. Pressing any key clears it; the stack and the registers keep their values, so after the fix you can usually compute again without re-entering everything.

Every message

MessageWhat it meansFix
Error 0Impossible math: division by zero, square root of a negative number, logarithm of zero or less, Δ% or %T from a base of zero, factorial of a non-integer.Check the number in the display (X) and the one before it (Y).
Error 1Storage register overflow: storage arithmetic (STO + and similar) gave a number beyond 9.999999999 × 10⁹⁹.Use smaller numbers or clear the register.
Error 2Statistics without enough data: a mean with no data, a standard deviation with one point, a linear estimate with no variation.Enter the data with Σ+ first (fCLEAR ΣSST clears it).
Error 3IRR found no answer or more than one is possible (flows that change sign more than once).Key a guess for the rate and press RCLgR/SPSE.
Error 4Program memory: more than 99 lines, a GTO to a line that does not exist, or storage arithmetic on a register that does not allow it.Check the program and its GTO targets.
Error 5The financial problem has no answer: PV, PMT and FV with the same sign, i at or below -100%, or a number of payments for AMORT that is not a positive whole number.Make the money you pay negative (CHS); check that n, i and the amounts make sense together.
Error 6Storage registers: a register that does not exist (used by program lines), more than 20 cash flows, or an Nj above 99.Split large Nj values; free registers by clearing the program (fCLEAR PRGMR↓ in program mode).
Error 7IRR with cash flows that never change sign (all positive or all negative).Enter the investment as negative: 50000CHSgPVCF₀.
Error 8Calendar: an impossible date or one out of range, or a date in the wrong format (M.DY vs D.MY).Check the format: g5M.DY M.DY, g4D.MY D.MY (D.MY shows on the display).
Error 9The self-test found a hardware problem.Take the calculator to service.
Pr ErrorContinuous Memory was reset: the batteries ran out or were changed too slowly, or the reset keys were pressed. Every register and program is cleared.Press any key; check the settings (decimals, END, date format) and re-enter your data.

Error 5, step by step

You borrowed 9,000 and pay 250 a month for 40 months; both amounts were entered as positive:

With the payment negative the rate is 0.52% a month. The same goes for FV: in a savings problem, the deposits (PMT) and the balance you take out (FV) must have opposite signs.
