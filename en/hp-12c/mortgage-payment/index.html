HP 12C mortgage payment, interest and balance (amortization)

Short answer

Clear the financial registers, enter the loan, the term and the rate, then press PMT: -1,896.20 a month (negative because it is money you pay).

fCLEAR FINx≷y·300000PV·30gn12×·6.5gi12÷·PMT

Numbers checked on 2026-11-23Works on: HP 12C, 12C Gold and 12C Platinum in RPN mode

📱 Try these keys on your phone: 12c Pro, free on Android →

The HP 12C solves loans with five keys: n (number of payments), i (rate per period), PV (amount borrowed), PMT (payment) and FV (what is left at the end). Enter any three or four and press the key you want. fAMORTn (AMORT) then splits the payments into interest and principal and gives the balance.

Example: payment on a 30-year mortgage

You borrow 300,000 dollars at 6.5% a year, compounded monthly, repaid over 30 years with a payment at the end of each month. What is the payment, how much of the first year goes to interest, and what do you still owe after a year?

The payment is -1,896.20. In the first 12 payments, -19,401.28 is interest and -3,353.12 repays principal; you still owe 296,646.88. The signs follow the cash: money you receive is positive, money you pay is negative.

gn12× (12×) and gi12÷ (12÷) do the monthly conversion for you: years become months in n and the yearly rate becomes a monthly rate in i. Typing 360n and the monthly rate in i gives the same result.

The rest of the schedule

AMORT continues from where it stopped: press 12fAMORTn again for year 2, then x≷y for its principal and RCLPV for the new balance. 1fAMORTn gives a single month. To start the schedule over, store the loan amount in PV again (and reset n if you need it).

For AMORT, the HP 12C rounds the payment and each month's interest to the number of decimals on the display, as a lender does in cents. Keep 2 decimals (f2) for money; with more decimals the results change by a few cents.

Down payment

PV is the amount you borrow, not the price: for a 375,000 home with 75,000 down, enter 300,000. The down payment changes the loan, not the keys.

Payment due at signing

If the first payment is due on the day the loan starts (common for leases and store installment plans), switch to BEGIN with g7BEG before pressing PMT, and back to END with g8END afterwards. BEGIN stays on until you turn it off, even when the calculator is switched off.
