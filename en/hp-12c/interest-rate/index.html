Find the interest rate of a loan or installment plan on the HP 12C

Short answer

Enter what you receive as positive PV, the payment as negative PMT and the number of payments in n, then press i: 0.52% per month.

fCLEAR FINx≷y·9000PV·250CHSPMT·40n·i

Work it out with your own numbers ↓

Works on: HP 12C, 12C Gold and 12C Platinum in RPN mode

📱 Try these keys on your phone: 12c Pro, free on Android →

To find a rate, give the HP 12C everything else: the amount (PV), the payment (PMT), the number of payments (n) and, if there is one, a final amount (FV). Then press i. One of PV and PMT must be negative, or the calculator shows Error 5.

Example: the rate on a loan

A lender gives you 9,000 today. You repay it with 40 monthly payments of 250, the first one a month from now. What rate are you paying?

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
1fCLEAR FINx≷y0.00Clear the financial registers.
29000PV9,000.00Amount received, positive.
3250CHSPMT-250.00Each payment, negative: you pay it.
440n40.00Number of monthly payments.
5i0.52Rate per month, in percent.

The loan costs 0.52% a month. Times 12 gives the nominal yearly rate (the APR on a US loan): 12× gives 6.29%. For the effective yearly rate, see compound interest.

The rate hidden in "0% financing"

A store sells a laptop for 2,000 in 10 monthly payments of 200 "with no interest", or for 1,800 cash. Paying in installments means giving up the 200 discount, and that has a rate:

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
1fCLEAR FINx≷y0.00Clear the financial registers.
21800PV1,800.00The cash price: what you really get.
3200CHSPMT-200.00Each installment, negative.
410n10.0010 installments, the first a month after buying.
5i1.96The monthly rate hidden in the installments.

The installments carry 1.96% a month. If the cash price is lower than the installment total, there is interest, whatever the ad says.

You seeWhyFix
0.00The installment total (2,000) went into PV. Without a cash discount the rate really is 0%; the interest is in the discount you give up.PV is the cash price you could pay instead.

When the first installment is paid at the checkout, use BEGIN. The rate is then higher, because you pay sooner:

With the first payment at the checkout the hidden rate is 2.42% a month.
