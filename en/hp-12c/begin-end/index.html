BEGIN vs END on the HP 12C: when to use each, and how to turn BEGIN off

g7BEG (BEG) puts payments at the start of each period and shows BEGIN; g8END (END) puts them at the end and removes it. Neither CLEAR nor switching off changes it.

Works on: HP 12C, 12C Gold and 12C Platinum in RPN mode · Numbers checked on 2026-11-23

Every TVM calculation on the HP 12C assumes the payments fall either at the end of each period (END, the normal case: loans, mortgages, most savings plans) or at the beginning (BEGIN: rent, leases, insurance, a store installment paid at the checkout). The word BEGIN on the display tells you which mode is on.

Example: the same deposits, END and BEGIN

How to turn BEGIN off

Press g8END (the blue END under the 8 key). BEGIN stays on through CLEAR FIN, CLEAR REG and switching the calculator off, so check the display before each problem. Only a full reset (Pr Error) also sets END.

Which one to use

SituationMode
Loan or mortgage, first payment a month after signingEND (g8END)
Savings with deposits at the end of each monthEND
Rent, lease, insurance paid in advanceBEGIN (g7BEG)
Installment plan with the first payment at purchaseBEGIN
Single amount (PMT = 0)Either: it makes no difference
