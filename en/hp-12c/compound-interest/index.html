Compound interest on the HP 12C: future value, present value and rate conversion

Short answer

Enter the amount invested as a negative PV, the number of periods in n and the rate per period in i, then press FV: 1,126.83.

fCLEAR FINx≷y·1000CHSPV·12n·1i·FV

Work it out with your own numbers ↓

Works on: HP 12C, 12C Gold and 12C Platinum in RPN mode

📱 Try these keys on your phone: 12c Pro, free on Android →

Compound interest on the HP 12C uses the same five keys as a loan: n, i, PV, PMT and FV. Leave PMT at zero (CLEAR FIN does that) when there are no regular deposits.

Future value of a single amount

You invest 1,000 at 1% a month. How much do you have after 12 months?

Converting a monthly rate to a yearly rate

A card or loan charges 2% a month. What is that per year, with interest on interest? Invest 100 for 12 months and see what it becomes:

StepKeysDisplay showsWhat it does
1fCLEAR FINx≷y0.00Clear the financial registers.
2100CHSPV-100.00Invest 100 (any amount works; 100 reads as percent).
312n12.0012 months in a year.
42i2.00The monthly rate.
5FV126.82What 100 grows to in a year.
6100−26.82Minus the 100: the effective yearly rate in %.

2% a month is 26.82% a year (effective), not 24%. The same keys with 365n and a daily rate convert a daily rate.

Converting a yearly rate to a monthly rate
