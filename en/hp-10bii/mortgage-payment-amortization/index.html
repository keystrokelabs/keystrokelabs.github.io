HP 10bII+ mortgage payment and amortization (P/YR, BEG/END)

With P/YR at 12, enter N in months, the yearly rate in I/YR and the loan in PV, then press PMT: -1,498.88. Then 1INPUT12orangeAMORTFV (AMORT) and = three times give the year's principal, interest and balance.

orangeC ALLC·12orangeP/YRPMT·360N·6I/YR·250000PV·PMT

Works on: HP 10bII+ (and the older 10bII) · Numbers checked on 2026-11-23

The HP 10bII+ TVM keys are N, I/YR, PV, PMT and FV. Type a number and press a key to store it; press a key alone to compute it. I/YR is always a yearly rate, divided by P/YR (payments per year) inside the calculator.

Example: a 30-year mortgage

A 250,000 mortgage at 6% a year, 30 years, monthly payments at the end of each month.

Notes

  • AMORT uses the payment rounded to the displayed decimals, as a lender does; keep 2 decimals for money.
  • orange×P/YRN (×P/YR) multiplies the number on the display by P/YR: 30orange×P/YRNN stores 360.
  • BEGIN on the display means payments at the start of each period; orangeBeg/EndMAR (BEG/END) switches.
