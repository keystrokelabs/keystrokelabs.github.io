About Keystroke Labs

Keystroke Labs is written by an independent developer who builds financial calculator apps for Android (published on Google Play as PokeVision). Building those apps meant re-creating every function of three calculators and testing them against their guidebooks; these pages share what that work turned up, especially the small mistakes that give a wrong answer.

Keystroke Labs is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Texas Instruments or HP. The calculator names are used only to say which calculator a page is about. There are no logos or artwork from either company on this site or in the apps.

Contact
